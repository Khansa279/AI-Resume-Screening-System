Department: Marketing & Brand Management
Location: Lahore, Pakistan

About BrightWave Consumer Brands

BrightWave Consumer Brands is a growing consumer-products company focused on building strong brands through customer-focused marketing, digital campaigns, and data-driven decision-making. We are looking for a Marketing Specialist to join our Marketing & Brand Management team.

Position Overview

The Marketing Specialist will be responsible for planning and executing digital marketing campaigns, developing content strategies, managing social media channels, analyzing campaign performance, and supporting the company's overall brand-growth strategy.

The successful candidate will combine creativity with analytical thinking and will work closely with designers, copywriters, sales teams, and external marketing agencies.

Key Responsibilities
Plan and execute integrated digital marketing campaigns.
Develop content strategies and editorial calendars.
Manage social media campaigns and content.
Plan, launch, and optimize Google Ads and Meta Ads campaigns.
Conduct market research, competitor analysis, and audience segmentation.
Monitor campaign KPIs including CTR, conversion rate, CPA, ROAS, and engagement.
Use Google Analytics 4 to analyze website traffic, customer behavior, and campaign performance.
Conduct A/B testing of advertisements, messaging, email campaigns, and landing pages.
Develop and manage email marketing campaigns.
Conduct keyword research and implement on-page SEO strategies.
Prepare monthly campaign reports and marketing presentations.
Coordinate with designers, copywriters, sales teams, and external agencies.
Identify opportunities to improve campaign performance and customer engagement.

Strong analytical and reporting skills.
Excellent communication and teamwork skills.
